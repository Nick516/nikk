FAQ

Questions we get roasted with.

Is the roast actually honest or just nice?
+
Brutally honest, but not cruel. The AI is trained to be like a very direct friend who actually knows design — it will tell you the overhead lighting is destroying your vibe without making you feel bad about yourself. The room is roasted, not the person.
What kind of photos work best?
+
Wide shots that show most of the room work best. Natural lighting helps but isn't required — in fact, if your lighting is bad, AI will definitely notice. Any photo from your phone works. The messier the room, the more interesting the roast.
Are my photos stored or used for training?
+
Your photos are processed securely and only stored in your personal history. They are never used to train AI models, never shared publicly without your explicit consent, and you can delete them anytime from your profile.
How does the AI redesign actually work?
+
After analysing your room, the AI generates a reimagined version using your actual space as a base — same layout, same window positions, but with the style applied. It's not a random template. It uses your specific room's dimensions and structure.
Can I roast rooms that aren't mine?
+
Technically yes. The AI doesn't verify ownership. People regularly roast their parents' living rooms, Airbnbs they're staying in, or rooms from real estate listings. We take no responsibility for any family arguments that result.
What if I get a high score?
+
The AI will congratulate you with the same level of energy it uses to roast everyone else. A 9/10 room gets told Architectural Digest should be calling. Screenshots are encouraged. Bragging is mandatory.
